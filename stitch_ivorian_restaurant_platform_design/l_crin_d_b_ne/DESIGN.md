## Brand & Style

The design system embodies contemporary African fine dining situated in the heart of Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire. It merges ancestral culinary heritage with modern gastronomic precision. The visual language evokes an organic, sun-warmed refinement—grounded in artisanal craftsmanship, earthenware textures, and rich botanical depths.

### Aesthetics & Tone
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, prestigious, inviting, and architecturally rooted in modern Ivorian culture.
- **Design Style:** Modern Editorial Tactile. The interface balances generous breathing room, high-fashion editorial typography, and delicate ceramic-inspired borders with subtle organic warmth.
- **Sensory Metaphor:** Hand-thrown terracotta plateware, lush tropical foliage of the Banco National Park, golden Ivorian sunlight, and raw cacao beans.
- **Audience:** Discerning local and international epicures, cultural connoisseurs, and hospitality guests seeking modern interpretations of authentic West African cuisine.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in natural earth tones and lush foliage, honoring Côte d'Ivoire's landscape and agricultural heritage while maintaining absolute digital accessibility and prestige.

### Color Roles & Application
- **Deep Forest Green (`#174C38`):** The primary identity color. Governs global headers, navigation items, brand badges, and dominant structural accents. Inspires stability and tropical richness.
- **Ivorian Orange (`#F58220`):** The vibrant secondary color. Used exclusively for high-intent interactive calls-to-action (Table Reservations, Confirmations, Primary Checkout triggers).
- **Soft Antique Gold (`#C6A15B`):** The tertiary accent. Denotes chef selections, tasting menu badges, VIP designations, decorative dividing rules, and star ratings.
- **Cocoa Brown (`#4A2E24`):** The deep neutral. Replaces harsh blacks for all typography, high-contrast borders, and deep shadow tinting.
- **Warm Ivory (`#FFF8ED`):** Canvas background. Emits sun-drenched hospitality and softens screen glare compared to clinical white.
- **Sand Beige (`#E8D8C3`):** Neutral surface boundary and divider color. Reminiscent of raw unglazed stoneware.
- **Terracotta (`#B85C38`):** Earthy accent for culinary highlights, ingredient tags, and seasonal event notices.

## Typography

The typographical pairing reflects a dialogue between heritage literature and modern culinary operational clarity.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles (Playfair Display):** Conveys the poise, tradition, and culinary prestige of a Michelin-caliber establishment. Display and Headline variants leverage italics occasionally for sensory French/Ivorian culinary terms (e.g., *Attiéké*, *Alloco*, *Sauce Graine*).
- **Body & Data Inputs (Inter):** Ensures legibility for menu descriptions, guest reservation workflows, allergy disclosures, and operating hours across dense layouts.
- **Currency & Pricing Display:** Prices are formatted strictly with the FCFA suffix using `Inter` Medium or SemiBold with tabular lining figures (e.g., `18 500 FCFA` with a non-breaking space).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces an editorial culinary structure using an asymmetrical 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single unified column on mobile.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapters
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) margins. Maximum container width capped at `1280px` for optimal reading comfort.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins.

### Spatial Hierarchy
- Menu sections and storytelling passages employ `space-2xl` (64px) vertical separation to mimic curated gallery exhibitions.
- Dish items within a category maintain `space-lg` (24px) internal grouping.
- Tight pairings between dish titles, French descriptions, and FCFA currency prices utilize `space-xs` and `space-sm` for immediate contextual cohesion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh drop shadows in favor of ambient, sun-filtered warmth that mimics natural daylight hitting ceramic glaze and linen tablecloths.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Base Canvas:** `#FFF8ED` (Warm Ivory) sets the resting environment.
- **Resting Surface:** Cards and raised surfaces utilize pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale sand (`#F9F1E2`) framed by a razor-thin border of Sand Beige (`#E8D8C3`, 1px solid).
- **Ambient Shadows:** All shadows are tinted with warm Cocoa Brown (`#4A2E24`), preventing sterile grays:
  - *Subtle Card Lift (Hover):* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(74, 46, 36, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(74, 46, 36, 0.04)`
  - *Reservation Modal & Drawers:* `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(74, 46, 36, 0.16)`
- **Artisanal Ceramic Borders:** Plates, tasting menus, and wine cards use a double border motif on primary cards: a 1px outer stroke of `#E8D8C3` paired with an inset hairline ring of `#C6A15B` (Soft Antique Gold) at 20% opacity.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects softly hand-finished ceramics, avoiding aggressive rounded curves while eschewing stark brutalist edges.

- **Level 1 (Soft):** 
  - Standard interactive elements (input fields, buttons, small cards) use a `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
  - Large menu cards and photography containers scale to `0.5rem` (8px).
  - Selected signature motifs (such as decorative botanical banners and artisanal badges) may utilize asymmetric organic corners (e.g., top-left/bottom-right curved at 16px, opposite corners at 4px) to subtly reference handcrafted clay dishes.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Dietary badges (e.g., Vegan, Gluten-Free, Halal) and status pills strictly retain full pill geometry (`9999px`) to distinguish meta-tags from clickable cards and buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Reservation / Order):** Solid Ivorian Orange (`#F58220`) background, crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), `Inter` font, semi-bold 14px uppercase with subtle letter tracking (`0.05em`). Padding: `12px 28px`. Hover: shifts to Terracotta (`#B85C38`) with smooth 200ms transition.
- **Secondary Action (View Menu, Sommelier Pairing):** Deep Forest Green (`#174C38`) outline (1.5px), transparent background, Deep Forest Green text. Hover: Solid `#174C38` fill with Ivory text (`#FFF8ED`).
- **Text Buttons:** Cocoa Brown (`#4A2E24`) text with an underline in Antique Gold (`#C6A15B`) positioned 4px below baseline.

### Menu Cards & Platter Showcases
- White (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Sand (`#F9F1E2`) surfaces enclosed in a 1px border of `#E8D8C3`.
- Imagery uses natural ratio (4:3 or 1:1) with a soft warm vignette.
- Top section: Playfair Display title paired with tabular FCFA price aligned to the right margin.
- Body: 2-line dish composition description in Cocoa Brown (`#4A2E24`, 80% opacity).
- Footer: Tag badges for key local ingredients (e.g., "Manihot Esculenta", "Kpakpo Shito", "Cacao Grand Cru").

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- Micro-pills with 1px border. Background `#FFF8ED`, text `#4A2E24`, border `#E8D8C3`.
- Active or highlighted badges (e.g., "Chef's Signature", "Grand Cru") utilize Soft Antique Gold fill (`#C6A15B`) with white text.

### Form Inputs & Reservation Selectors
- Background: Surface White (`#FFFFFF`).
- Resting Border: 1px `#E8D8C3`.
- Active / Focus: 1.5px solid Deep Forest Green (`#174C38`), no glowing blue halos.
- Label: Inter Medium (12px), Cocoa Brown (`#4A2E24`), positioned above the field.
- Date/Guest Pickers: Calendar highlights styled in Deep Forest Green and Ivorian Orange.

### Currency Formatting Component
- Always renders the full numerical amount followed by `FCFA`.
- Formatted with thousands separator space: `24 000 FCFA`.
- Rendered in Inter SemiBold with `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` to ensure uniform alignment in tabular tasting menus.
