## Brand & Style

This design system mediates between high-end West African fine dining and mission-critical enterprise hospitality management. It serves executive chefs, floor sommeliers, general managers, and finance directors orchestrating multi-room seatings, cellar inventories, and live service pacing in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire.

The design philosophy fuses **Tactile Editorial Luxury** with **High-Density Enterprise SaaS**. Navigation frames and persistent system chrome utilize disciplined matte obsidian and warm gold leaf metallics, evoking physical brass menu clips and textured leather bill presenters. In direct contrast, operational work areas (Kitchen Display Systems, table floor plans, reservation matrices, and XOF financial ledgers) utilize high-readability ivory and pristine paper tones, ensuring rapid legibility under varying ambient dining room light or bustling kitchen conditions. The interface balances poise and rapid operational execution: zero visual noise, razor-sharp metric hierarchies, and deliberate gold accents that signify culinary authority.

## Colors

The palette establishes an architectural split between framing chrome and data-dense operational surfaces.

- **Primary (`#111111`)**: Deep matte obsidian used for sidebars, global command bars, critical action triggers, and primary typography across light workspaces. Anchored by pure foundational black (`#080808`) on extreme chrome perimeters.
- **Secondary (`#C6A15B`) & Warm Gold (`#D9B56D`)**: Metallic aurum reserved for active navigation indicators, table status VIP tiers, brand monogram emblems, active selection borders, and focus rings. Never applied indiscriminately to large surface areas.
- **Tertiary (`#174C38`)**: Heritage forest green used for positive settlement, kitchen dispatch completion, and VIP guest validation. Augmented by operational emerald (`#10B981`) for micro-indicators and live table status pulses.
- **Neutral (`#FFF8ED`)**: Warm linen-ivory that forms the canvas base, preventing sterile coldness. Layered with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated data cards, table cells, and active modal overlays.
- **Operational Status Palette**:
  - *Pending / En Préparation*: Terracotta amber (`#B85C38` base, `#F59E0B` micro-accent).
  - *Urgent / Table Alert / Annulé*: Deep crimson (`#EF4444`).
  - *Coursier / Information / Dispatch*: Deep cobalt (`#2563EB`).
  - *Border Line*: Hairline brass-tinted neutral (`#E8E0D2`) across data tables; matte border (`#262626`) across dark chrome.

## Typography

The typographical tension pairing balances French-Ivorian haute-cuisine refinement with rapid-scan numerical ergonomics:

- **Playfair Display**: Used exclusively for prominent contextual anchors, service summaries, page titles, restaurant zone designations (e.g., *Salon Ebène*, *Terrasse Lagune*), and luxury customer greeting headers.
- **Inter**: Drives all operational controls, POS entry fields, order modifier trees, timestamping, and inventory data. Numerical weights leverage tabular figures (`tnum`, `cv01`) to align large West African Franc figures (`FCFA 2,450,000`) cleanly without visual drift across rows.
- **Labeling Discipline**: Small indicators and status badges run in uppercase `label-md` and `label-sm` with expanded tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`), ensuring immediate legibility on low-brightness handheld terminals and kitchen print tickets.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an asymmetrical enterprise shell:
- **Navigation Dock & Global Controls**: Persistent 260px matte-black rail pinned on the left for desktop, collapsing to an icon-driven 64px rail on tablet landscape and off-canvas bottom navigation on mobile.
- **Data Workspaces**: Fluid 12-column analytical canvas using adaptive gutters. Margins compress from 32px on wide desktop monitors (reservation Gantt charts and multi-table POS overviews) to 14px on floor-manager tablets.
- **Spatial Rhythm**: Multi-element lists (kitchen item lines, inventory counts, ticket additions) adhere to strict vertical intervals calibrated in multiples of 4px and 7px base baselines. Tables emphasize horizontal readability with compact row heights (36px for dense transaction logs, 52px for active order flows with modifiers).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes crisp editorial discipline rather than heavy diffused blur effects:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - *Tier 0 (Root Chrome)*: `#080808` and `#111111` backdrop planes.
  - *Tier 1 (Canvas)*: `#FFF8ED` muted neutral ivory floor.
  - *Tier 2 (Work Cards & POS Tables)*: Crisp `#FFFFFF` elevated with low-contrast framing outlines (`1px solid #E8E0D2`).
  - *Tier 3 (Active Orders & Floating Modals)*: Pure `#FFFFFF` elevated via subtle, warm-tinted ambient occlusion: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.06), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.08)`.
- **Gold Focus Depth**: Selected tables, active covers, and focused bill reconciliations receive a fine `1px` inner boundary coupled with a soft `0 0 0 1px #C6A15B` warm metallic ring.
- **Glass Accents**: Used strictly for dismissible floating floor filters and waiter service notices: `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a `1px solid rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.25)` edge.

## Shapes

The interface embraces an architectural, slightly softened square aesthetic (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Default elements (data cards, table headers, table cells, metric containers) utilize `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radiuses, reflecting crisp print menus and ledger layouts.
- Intermediate cards, order ticket modules, and contextual drawer surfaces step up to `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- Only pill elements (e.g., table status chips, live kitchen timers, course markers) use fully continuous radial rounding (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish active status states from structural content cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary Luxury Action**: Solid `#111111` fill with `#FFF8ED` text, a hairline `1px solid #C6A15B` border, and `4px` radius. On hover, background shifts to pure obsidian `#000000` with gold text glow.
- **Secondary (Accented)**: Crisp ivory `#FFF8ED` with a `1px solid #C6A15B` metallic border, `#111111` typography, and golden hover fill (`rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.12)`).
- **Destructive**: Hairline `#EF4444` border with subtle crimson-tinted background (`#FEF2F2`) and `#EF4444` text; shifts to high-contrast red fill under deliberate active press.

### Status Chips & Course Indicators
- Capsule-rounded (`9999px`), `22px` height, uppercase `label-sm` Inter font.
- **Payé / Terminé**: Pale forest background (`#E6F4EA`), deep forest green text (`#174C38`), and a continuous `6px` emerald pulse dot.
- **En Préparation / Cuisine**: Light amber background (`#FEF3C7`), rich terracotta text (`#B85C38`).
- **Urgent / Retard**: Crimson wash (`#FEE2E2`) with `#B91C1C` text and high-contrast alert icon.
- **Service Coursier / Externe**: Deep blue wash (`#EFF6FF`) with `#1D4ED8` label.

### Data Tables & Ledger Grids
- **Header**: `#FAF4E8` warm tint, `36px` height, uppercase `label-md` tracking, separated by `1px solid #E8E0D2`.
- **Data Rows**: `#FFFFFF` with alternating subtle stripe (`#FCF9F2`), `44px` compact row height. Figures formatted in Inter `data-mono-num` with right-aligned currency formatting (`FCFA`).
- **Active Row Selection**: `1px solid #C6A15B` perimeter outline with an ultra-light gold ambient surface (`rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.05)`).

### Input Fields & Service Modifiers
- Background `#FFFFFF` set against `#FFF8ED` canvas.
- Crisp `1px solid #DDD3C1` structural border; on focus, border turns directly to `#C6A15B` with an absolute zero-offset gold halo.
- Labels use Inter `label-md` seated tightly above input fields with auxiliary notes formatted in `body-sm`.

### Operational Domain Components
- **Floor Plan Table Node**: Squared cards with `4px` corners showing table identification, assigned headwaiter, course stage progress bars, and aggregated seating time. Outlined in metallic gold for reserved VIP seatings.
- **Kitchen Display Ticket (KDS)**: High-contrast white cards framed in matte obsidian `#111111`, top-stamped with course markers (Entrée, Plat, Dessert), item allergies in crimson pill tags, and exact elapsed kitchen chronometers.
- **XOF Revenue Ribbon**: Top-line summary widget displaying current service intake, average check size, and open covers utilizing `Playfair Display` for totals and `Inter` tabular figures for velocity comparisons.
